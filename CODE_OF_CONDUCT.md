# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and constructive; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, ask clarifying questions, and explain the reasoning behind
  review comments.
- Respect differing deployment constraints, language preferences, and levels of
  familiarity with the codebase.
- Keep discussions on topic and move sensitive or personal matters to a private
  channel.
- Give credit for ideas and work that come from others.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory comments.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing private information (doxxing) without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Retaliating against someone for reporting a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
comments, and any project space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer listed in
[CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS) through GitHub. Do not use public issues for
conduct reports. Include what happened, where, and any supporting context.

Maintainers will acknowledge reports within a reasonable time, keep them
confidential, and may take any of the following actions:

1. A private clarification or warning.
2. Removal of a comment or rejection of a contribution.
3. Temporary or permanent restriction from project spaces.
4. Escalation to the hosting platform when required.

Decisions are made in good faith and may be appealed once by replying to the
private report. Maintainers involved in an incident recuse themselves from the
decision when possible.
